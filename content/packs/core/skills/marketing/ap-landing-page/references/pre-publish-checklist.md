# Landing-page pre-publish checklist

Use the relevant checks. Mark anything not exercised or not applicable rather
than assuming it passed.

## Strategy and message

- The page has one primary audience, offer, and action.
- The promise matches the actual traffic source and visitor awareness.
- A qualified visitor can identify the product, fit, outcome, and next step
  without inference.
- The mechanism explains how the outcome is produced.
- Differentiation is concrete and supported by the product.
- The page distinguishes current capabilities from roadmap or waitlist claims.
- Copy contains no vague hype, placeholder language, or unsupported
  superlatives.

## Proof and trust

- Every statistic, customer, quotation, logo, certification, integration, and
  capability is verified.
- Proof appears near the claim it supports.
- The most important objections are answered before the final action.
- Pricing, trial, cancellation, qualification, and next-step terms are clear
  where applicable.
- Security, privacy, reliability, migration, and compatibility information
  appears where the audience needs it.
- The page does not use fake badges, unnamed testimonials, or irrelevant logos.

## Conversion path

- One primary action is visually and verbally clear.
- CTA text explains what the visitor receives or what happens next.
- Secondary actions do not compete with the primary one.
- The form asks only for information the next step requires.
- Validation, error, loading, success, and confirmation states work.
- Conversion tracking records the intended action once and with the correct
  context.
- The post-conversion experience fulfills the promise.

## Content and discovery

- Title, meta description, H1, headings, and internal links match the page's
  real intent when the page is indexed.
- Important entities and facts are explicit and current.
- Indexed variants provide genuinely different utility.
- Structured data, when present, describes visible content and validates.
- Canonical, robots, sitemap, and indexing behavior match the page strategy.
- The page does not rely on keyword stuffing, thin FAQs, doorway variants, or
  AI-specific markup myths.

## Experience and delivery

- The rendered page was reviewed at narrow/mobile and representative desktop
  widths.
- Primary content, forms, navigation, media, and fixed elements do not overflow
  or obscure one another.
- Keyboard operation, visible focus, labels, alternative text, contrast, and
  reduced motion were checked.
- Images, video, fonts, and animation are purposeful and appropriately loaded.
- Relevant build, tests, lint, type checks, and browser-console review pass.
- Performance was measured with appropriate lab or field data; the result is
  labeled correctly.

## Measurement

- The primary success metric reflects a qualified business outcome.
- Guardrails cover quality, activation, retention, refunds, trust, or other
  downstream risks as relevant.
- Experiment variants change a meaningful hypothesis rather than arbitrary
  decoration.
- Analytics, search reporting, and attribution needed for evaluation are in
  place before launch.
- The release or experiment owner knows when and how the result will be
  reviewed.
